Recommendations

Actionable steps to grow profit and reduce risk

1 Confirm Sep 2026 invoices

Load any invoices after 1 Sep so the dashboard reflects the current position.

2 Lean into charms

Best profit/unit (₹98) & highest margins (Bal Ganesha 89%, Spidey 86%, Virat 82%). Add new characters, test 3-charm bundles.

3 Fix thin Bloom items

Test ₹60 or sell in packs. Add a ₹10 customization fee on 42 units → +₹420.

4 Hold list prices

Check Lustreline Choker (₹50 vs ₹150 list). Rule: never sell below cost + 50%.

5 Make discounts follow margin

Bloom & charms can afford 20% off (stays near 60%). Bags: smaller discounts. Review monthly vs profit.

6 Keep ₹100 outstation fee

Covers highest real cost (₹90). Bundle/minimum order so a single ₹85 charm doesn't carry ₹100 delivery. Record every delivery cost.

7 Follow up repeat buyers

Nudge ~2 weeks after each order. Start with Jul–Aug customers who haven't returned.

8 Record true costs

Packaging, labour time, ad spend — so the report shows true net profit.

Product Roadmap · Inventory & Stock

Why it's worth building — and how to start

Bags tie up real money. A batch of 20 Pearl Bead Bags costs ₹6,340 — about the same as all product cost sold in 12 months (₹6,529). Only 2 sold in that time.
Charms are low risk. Cost ₹15–₹30 each — stock mistakes are cheap.
Demand comes in waves. Reorder levels should use last 4–8 weeks, not lifetime sales. Pure Petal: 19 in Jan–Feb, none since March.

Suggested rollout

Phase 1: Track ready-made standard items — all bags + top movers (Virat charm, Lavender Loop, Rosy Twist, Sunshine Bloom Clip, Cotton Candy Bow, Hangin' Spidey).
Phase 2: Remaining products.
Phase 3: Raw materials (beads, yarn).
Customized items (42 units) are made-to-order — do not stock.
